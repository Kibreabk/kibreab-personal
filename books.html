Sigmund Freud

Dream Psychology: Psychoanalysis for Beginners

A doorway into dreams, symbols, and the unconscious mind.

An introduction to Freud's ideas about dream interpretation, the unconscious mind, symbolism, and wish fulfillment—looking at dreams as expressions of thoughts and desires that may not be obvious while we are awake.

Shelf two

Finished Books

Six covers, five finished reads, and no star ratings. The takeaway matters more than the score.

01 / FINISHED

Pride and Prejudice

Jane Austen

A sharp story about first impressions, pride, social expectations, and personal growth. Beneath the romance is a clever examination of how quickly people judge one another—and how wrong those judgments can be.

02 / FINISHED

The Alchemist

Ben Jonson

The 1610 satirical play—not Paulo Coelho's novel.

A witty satire built around greed, deception, and people's willingness to believe almost anything when promised wealth or advantage. Jonson turns human foolishness into the engine of the entire story.

03 / FINISHED

The Old Man and the Sea

Ernest Hemingway

Santiago's struggle with the marlin becomes much more than a story about fishing. It is a compact story about endurance, pride, dignity, defeat, and continuing to fight even when there may be nothing left to gain.

04 / FINISHED

Of Mice and Men

John Steinbeck

George and Lennie hold onto a simple dream of having a place of their own while living through an unforgiving world. Their friendship gives the story its warmth—and makes where it ultimately goes hit considerably harder.

05 / FINISHED

Animal Farm

George Orwell

A sharp political allegory about power, corruption, propaganda, and how revolutions can betray the ideals that started them. Simple on the surface, brutal underneath.
