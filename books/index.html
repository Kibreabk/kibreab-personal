Currently reading

Sigmund Freud

Dream Psychology:
Psychoanalysis for Beginners

Freud approaches dreams as a route into the unconscious, arguing that their strange images can hide wishes, fears, and unresolved conflicts. The reading is less about a neat dream dictionary and more about learning how desire, memory, and symbolic substitution shape the mind’s night-time stories.

Finished books

Closed covers. Open arguments.

Jane Austen

Pride and Prejudice

Austen turns romance into a sharp study of social class, reputation, and the danger of deciding who someone is too quickly. Elizabeth and Darcy’s growth makes the book feel less like a simple love story and more like an elegant argument for self-awareness.

Finished

Ben Jonson · 1610 satire play

The Alchemist

Not the Paulo Coelho novel: Jonson’s comedy follows a household of con artists feeding on the greed and fantasies of Renaissance London. Its deceptions are fast, funny, and painfully recognizable—a reminder that people are often most vulnerable when a lie promises exactly what they want.
